# Privacy

Effective 4 September 2026.

This plugin is a collection of instructions and document templates. It operates within the AI application you choose. It has no publisher-operated server, account system, analytics, telemetry, external connector, hooks or credential storage. The distributed package contains no private school or student information.

The AI host processes your prompts, attachments, generated documents and any web or file-tool activity under that host's policies and your account settings. The publisher does not receive that content through this plugin. Do not supply confidential or identifying student information unless your organisation and host settings permit it.

If you open a public GitHub issue, the information you post is public and is processed by GitHub. Remove personal information, confidential documents and credentials before posting. See [support](SUPPORT.md).
