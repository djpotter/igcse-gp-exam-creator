# Specimen Question Templates

**IMPORTANT:** Use these EXACT formats when creating question papers. These are based on the Cambridge 2025 Specimen Paper (0457/01/SP/25) and verified against all 2025 examination papers.

---

## Question 1 [18 marks]

```
1   Study Sources 1 and 2.

    (a) According to Source 1, [specific factual question requiring a number or fact]?
                                                                                    [1]

    (b) (i) Identify one example of a [critical thinking concept] from Source 2.
                                                                                    [1]

        (ii) Explain why the example you identified is a [critical thinking concept].
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            ........................................................................ [2]

    (c) From Source 2, describe [organisation name]'s perspective on [topic].
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [12 lines provided]
            ........................................................................ [6]

    (d) Sources 1 and 2 suggest [causes/effects/factors] of [topic].

        Which [cause/effect/factor] do you think is the most significant? Explain why.
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [16 lines provided]
            ........................................................................ [8]
```

### Q1 Format Rules

1. **Q1(a):** Simple factual retrieval from Source 1 (number, percentage, or specific fact)
2. **Q1(b)(i):** Pre-specifies the concept - students FIND an example, not identify what type
3. **Q1(b)(ii):** Students explain WHY it is that concept (definition-based)
4. **Q1(c):** NO ICEVA scaffolding in the question - simple "describe perspective" format
5. **Q1(d):** Two sentences maximum - "Sources suggest X. Which is most significant? Explain why."

### Q1 Variations by Topic

**For causes:**
> "Sources 1 and 2 suggest causes of [topic]. Which cause do you think is the most significant? Explain why."

**For effects:**
> "Sources 1 and 2 suggest effects of [topic]. Which effect do you think is the most significant? Explain why."

**For factors:**
> "Sources 1 and 2 suggest factors that [relate to topic]. Which factor do you think is the most significant? Explain why."

### Critical Thinking Concepts for Q1(b)

Choose ONE per exam:
- Generalisation
- Prediction
- Claim
- Opinion
- Value
- Bias
- Vested interest

## Question 2 [16 marks]

```
2   Study Source 3.

    (a) Explain the strengths and weaknesses of the research outlined in Source 3.
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [16 lines provided]
            ........................................................................ [8]

    (b) '[Direct quote of a claim from Source 3].'

        Explain how this claim could be tested. You should consider the research
        methods and evidence that could be used.
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [16 lines provided]
            ........................................................................ [8]
```

### Q2 Format Rules

1. **Q2(a):** Always "Explain the strengths AND weaknesses" - both required
2. **Q2(b):** Claim in single quotation marks, directly quoted from Source 3
3. **Q2(b):** Standard instruction text: "Explain how this claim could be tested. You should consider the research methods and evidence that could be used."

### Q2(b) Claim Format

The claim should be:
- A direct quote from Source 3's conclusion
- A generalising statement that can be empirically tested
- Placed in single quotation marks
- Usually starts with "Most...", "People who...", "[Group] tend to...", etc.

**Examples from Cambridge papers:**
- 'Most people enjoy growing their own food.'
- 'Young people who have personal contact with migrants tend to have more positive attitudes toward migration.'
- 'People who shop online tend to spend more money than those who shop in stores.'

## Question 3 [16 marks]

```
3   Study Source 4.

    Which argument is more convincing, [Speaker A]'s or [Speaker B]'s?

    Your answer should:
    •   consider both arguments
    •   evaluate their reasoning, evidence and use of language
    •   support your judgement with their words and ideas.
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [22 lines provided]
            ...................................................................... [16]
```

### Q3 Format Rules

1. **Question stem:** "Which argument is more convincing, [Name]'s or [Name]'s?"
2. **Exactly THREE bullet points** - no more, no less
3. **Bullet point wording is FIXED** - use exactly as shown above

**DO NOT add bullets about:**
- "comparing alternatives"
- "discussing advantages and limitations"
- "reaching a conclusion" (implicit in "judgement")
- "using your own ideas"

## Question 4 [20 marks]

```
4   [Context sentence about what a government/organisation wants to achieve.]

    The following actions are being considered:
    •   [Action A - short description]
    •   [Action B - short description]
    •   [Action C - short description]

    Which one of these actions would you recommend to [the government/organisation],
    and why?

    In your answer, you should:
    •   state your recommendation
    •   give reasons and evidence to support your choice
    •   use the material in the sources and/or any of your own ideas
    •   consider different arguments and perspectives.
            ...........................................................................
            ...........................................................................
            [40 lines provided across 2 pages]
            ...................................................................... [20]
```

### Q4 Format Rules

1. **Context sentence:** One sentence establishing who wants to achieve what
2. **Three actions:** Simple, distinct options (not overly detailed)
3. **Question stem:** "Which one of these actions would you recommend to [X], and why?"
4. **Exactly FOUR bullet points** - no more, no less
5. **Bullet point wording is FIXED** - use exactly as shown above

**DO NOT add bullets about:**
- "explaining how it addresses the issue"
- "discussing advantages and limitations of each action"
- "comparing your recommendation with alternatives"

### Q4 Context Sentence Examples

- "A government wants to reduce hunger in its country."
- "An international organisation wants to increase access to education."
- "A local council wants to reduce the environmental impact of shopping."

### Q4 Action Examples

**Hunger topic (specimen):**
- Provide free food in areas of poverty.
- Invest in new farming technologies.
- Train people to grow and sell food in local areas.

**Migration topic:**
- Launch a public awareness campaign about the benefits of migration.
- Add migration topics to the school curriculum.
- Fund community integration programs.

**Climate change topic:**
- Introduce a carbon tax on businesses.
- Invest in renewable energy research.
- Ban single-use plastics.

**Education topic:**
- Build more schools in rural areas.
- Provide scholarships for disadvantaged students.
- Train more teachers and increase their pay.
